4. BARTER REGRESSION THEORY — STATIC FORMULA

Source: "Economic Dynamics (Part 2) — meatus ad aequilibrium et commercia terrae" (2024)

This formula is frozen. It does not change. Only the data changes.

─────────────────────────────────────────

Step 1 — Normalize to Metric Ton

Venue          | Unit            | Conversion to Metric Ton
---------------|-----------------|-------------------------
COMEX (CME)    | troy ounce      | × 32,150.7466
MCX (India)    | 10 grams        | × 100,000
SGE (China)    | gram            | × 1,000,000
MOEX (Russia)  | gram            | × 1,000,000

─────────────────────────────────────────

Step 2 — Convert to USD Using Central Bank FX

For each venue i, apply the official central bank exchange rate to convert
the local venue price into USD per metric ton.

Step 3 — Calculate Global Gold Price (Denominator)

Average the USD/ton figures across all active venues to produce the
daily global denominator.

Step 4 — Derive Barter Exchange Rates

For any target currency C:
  Barter Rate(C) = Global Gold Price (USD) ÷ Gold Price (C/ton)

Step 5 — Compare to Market FX

  Deviation = |Barter Rate − Market FX Rate| ÷ Market FX Rate × 100

⚠ Signal Threshold: Deviation > 2% → Barter Regression Opportunity.
